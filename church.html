Church

Church is a probabilistic programming language used for defining generative models and performing inference. It is based on the Lisp programming language and provides a powerful framework for expressing complex probabilistic models.

In Church, you can define random variables, specify their distributions, and perform inference to compute probabilities and make predictions. It supports various inference algorithms, including Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods and variational inference(s).

Church is often used in fields such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, and statistics for tasks like modeling uncertainty, making predictions, and understanding complex data.
